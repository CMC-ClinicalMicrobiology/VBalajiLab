Professional profile

Dr. Balaji Veeraraghavan is a clinical microbiologist and antimicrobial-resistance researcher with three decades of work in infectious-disease diagnostics, ESKAPE and enteric-fever genomics, evaluation of novel antimicrobial agents, and translational pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics. He has served as Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editor of the Indian Journal of Medical Microbiology and has sustained peer-review and grant-review responsibilities across national and international journals and funding agencies.

He is Principal Investigator of the ICMR Nodal Centre for Genomic AMR Surveillance and founded India’s first Hollow Fibre Infection Model platform for PK/PD-guided antimicrobial development.

Current appointments and qualifications

  • Senior Professor and Head, Department of Clinical Microbiology, Christian Medical College, Vellore — Head since 2012; Professor since 2008.
  • Hilda Lazarus Core Research Chair, CMC Vellore.
  • MD in Microbiology, 1999, and PhD in Basic Science, 2006 — Christian Medical College, Vellore.
  • Postdoctoral Fellow in Infectious Disease, Tufts Medical School, Boston, 2008–2009.
  • Fellow of the Royal College of Physicians, London, 2018.

Research leadership and funding

  • Principal Investigator, ICMR Nodal Centre for Antimicrobial Resistance Research and Surveillance, focusing on genomic AMR surveillance for non-fermenting Gram-negative and diarrhoeal pathogens.
  • Principal Investigator, ICMR in vitro Hollow Fibre Infection Model for combination PK/PD of generic and novel Gram-negative-active antibiotics — India’s first such platform.
  • Co-Principal Investigator, ICMR Advanced Research Centre for Infectious Diseases, 2025–2029, supporting AMR research, PK/PD-informed treatment optimisation, and research-capacity building.
  • Principal or Co-Principal Investigator on international programmes with the MRC (UK), Wellcome Sanger Institute, University of Cambridge, NUS Singapore, and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation on enteric fever, pneumococcal genomics, and Klebsiella genomics.
  • National reference laboratory for WHO for typhoidal Salmonella, Corynebacterium diphtheriae, and Bordetella pertussis; National EQAS provider in microbiology for more than 1,200 laboratories in India.

Editorial experience

  • Editor-in-Chief, Indian Journal of Medical Microbiology, 2015–2018 — led editorial policy, peer-review management, and strategic direction.
  • Associate Editor, Indian Journal of Medical Microbiology, 2012–2015.
  • Editor, Journal of Patient Safety and Infection Control.
  • Guest Editor and editorial contributor for Frontiers in Medicine and Frontiers in Microbiology collections on biofilm-mediated nosocomial infection and AMR.
  • Editor or contributing author for Ananthanarayan and Paniker’s Textbook of Microbiology, the CMC Hospital Infection Control Manual, and ICMR Hospital Infection Control Guidelines.
