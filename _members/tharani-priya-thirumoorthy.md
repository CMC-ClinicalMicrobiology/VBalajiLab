I am a researcher interested in microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, epidemiology, and public health, with a focus on typhoidal Salmonella. My doctoral research involved identifying two high-risk clones, understanding the role of plasmids in transferring AMR genes, and using environmental surveillance as an alternate approach to estimate community-level burden of S. Typhi.

**Research focus:** I currently work on transcriptomics to study the fitness effects produced by plasmids in S. Typhi, along with the isolation and genomic analysis of S. Typhi-specific bacteriophages.
