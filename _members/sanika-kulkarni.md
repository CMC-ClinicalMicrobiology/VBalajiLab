I am a researcher in clinical microbiology investigating the genomic mechanisms behind high-risk bacterial pathogens. Based in the Department of Clinical Microbiology at CMC Vellore, my doctoral work bridges large-scale bacterial genomics with translational diagnostic development. Specifically, I investigate the genetic convergence of multidrug resistance and hypervirulence in _Klebsiella pneumoniae_, leveraging population-level genomic insights to design targeted multiplex PCR assays for regional surveillance. Outside the laboratory, I enjoy literature, traveling, and exploring regional cuisines.

**Research focus:** _Klebsiella pneumoniae_ genomics & pathotypes , Molecular diagnostic panel design , AMR & hypervirulence co-occurrence , Regional genomic surveillance
