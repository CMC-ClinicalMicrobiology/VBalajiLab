I am a researcher with expertise in bacterial genetics, antimicrobial resistance, and molecular biology. At CMC, my work focuses on genomic surveillance of clinically important bacterial pathogens, molecular diagnostics, and the use of whole-genome sequencing to understand pathogen diversity and antimicrobial resistance.

**Research focus:** My research primarily focuses on _Burkholderia pseudomallei_, particularly its molecular detection, genomic characterization, and epidemiology. I am also interested in understanding the distribution of virulence and antimicrobial resistance determinants in clinically important bacterial pathogens.
